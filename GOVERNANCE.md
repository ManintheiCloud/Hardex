# Governance

Hardex is in its founding phase. This document describes how decisions are made now and how that is expected to evolve.

## Roles

- **Contributors** — anyone who opens an issue, reviews, or submits a change.
- **Maintainers** — contributors with merge rights, listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Maintainers review and merge changes, triage issues and security reports, and cut releases.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day changes are decided by maintainer review on pull requests (one approval; two for the sensitive areas listed in CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Architectural decisions are recorded as [ADRs](docs/adr/) and proposed by pull request, open for comment for at least 7 days.
- Disagreements are resolved by lazy consensus among maintainers; if consensus fails, by a simple majority of maintainers.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contributions and reviews over several months, nominated by an existing maintainer and approved by a majority of maintainers.

## Evolution

Once the project has maintainers from at least three independent organisations, a steering structure and a vendor-neutral home will be considered.
